Clarity pass — 2026-10-07

Design shared before implementation:
1. Lead seed cards with cost, growth time and price position; disclose estimates.
2. Give the opening one short action chain at a time.
3. Make planting mode and valid empty soil unmistakable.
4. Lead sale quotes with revenue and net profit; retain breakdowns by tapping.

Implemented using existing renderers, selection and controls:
- Compact seed cards and explicit Go to Farm navigation on mobile.
- Correct 2-day first-carrot display; subsequent carrots show their normal
  6 watered days. No change to growing logic, economy, saved IDs or ordering.
- Projections, crop cycle/noise/shock traits and descriptions in native details.
- Near/below/above average replaces ambiguous fair/discount/premium cues;
  neutral styling applies to both buying and selling. Cycle direction remains
  model-based, with visible uncertainty about the eventual price.
- Short action guidance, Plant label during selection, temporary dashed empty
  soil targets, small seed marker and an explicitly labeled cancel action.
  Repeated planting still works; Water/Mine still clear seed selection.
- Growing crop selection says Needs water or Watered; Rest stays accessible.
- Sale value and net profit lead, with cost and base price secondary.
- Fixed navigation and disclosure clicks clearing crop selection. Explicit
  close still clears it. Open disclosures survive same-selection UI refreshes.
- Farm/close/sale controls have 44px mobile targets. The legacy important
  close-width rule and general button sizing needed specific overrides.

Actual local Chromium testing with Playwright, portrait touch emulation:
320x740, 375x812, 390x844, 430x932 (height greater than width throughout).
At all sizes the primary Farm button was 44px tall and above Rest; close
controls measured at least 44x44; no horizontal overflow. Main seed-card
visible text at 390x844 fell from 85 to 33 whitespace-separated words.
Additional information remained accessible via a disclosure.
