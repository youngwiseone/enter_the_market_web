Water growth and crop costs (2026-10-07)

Watering earns its normal growth day at the next dawn. Water Retention keeps
moisture available for one additional day per stack after the last watering;
each completed retained day is stored in gridPlacedMeta.growthWater.retainedDays.
growthWater.throughDay prevents the same interval from being counted twice.
Earned days are captured before manual water, rain or sprinkler water updates
the last-water date, and at dawn for both farms. Manual water and sprinklers skip
currently retained moisture; rain may refresh it. Rewatering never removes
earned growth. The existing watering-can/sprinkler bonus growth roll is separate.

An older crop without growthWater initially has exactly the progress the former
formula displayed for its current last-water date. That visible contribution is
captured on the next action/dawn. We cannot reconstruct retained days that older
versions already erased. Migration does not guess those days or change cash.
All metadata travels in the existing farm arrays, so moves and farm switches
retain growth, rarity, opening exceptions, paid costs and transaction identity.

Each new treatment records the actual amount paid in fertiliser.paidCost.
Crop cost is its recorded seed acquisition cost plus all known treatment spend.
Reusable sprinklers are infrastructure and contribute no per-crop treatment cost.
Inventory placements use the inventory's recorded average acquisition cost.

Older fertiliser stacks without a recorded paidCost are historicalCostUnknown.
New paid applications add their exact amount, retaining that unknown flag. Quotes
and receipts display known-cost profit with an explicit historical limitation;
they never reconstruct spending from today's prices. Existing cash, prior sales
and historical records receive no retroactive charge, refund or rewritten profit.

Quality Fertiliser only affects the eventual rarity roll. Once a crop matures or
has a cached rarity, applications fail before cash or energy is spent. Growing,
unresolved crops can still stack quality. Utility descriptions describe real
effects and current prices, and legacy saved descriptions are refreshed by ID.

Regression command:
  node js/dev/tests/growth_costs.test.mjs
Requires modern Node supporting native ES modules in .js (Node 22+).
