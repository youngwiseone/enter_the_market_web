Balance concerns and remaining verification
- First sale moved from Day 9 to Day 3 in these runs, not a measured population average. Starting watering and a cleared tile reduce opening friction; subsequent mining still takes ten hits and ordinary carrots six growth days.
- Common crops retain their existing 1.2 multiplier; rare/mythic bonuses can outweigh a weak market. Correcting bulk quotes slightly raises their payouts to the existing individual-sale formula. No broader economy tuning attempted.
- Real phones, Safari/Firefox, narrow 320px screens, long sessions, all late-game progression/cosmetics/sprinkler combinations and live GitHub Pages are unverified. Farm switch/move preservation was verified through production functions, not a late-game browser unlock playthrough. Loss retry was tested with controlled state, not a naturally losing fresh browser harvest.
- No publishing/deployment performed.

Save compatibility safeguard
The game uses multiple unversioned localStorage keys, including both farms and legacy grid data. Changing the first saved/default item can trigger an inventory/shop reset: preserve IDs and ordering. Never treat a missing tutorial flag as evidence of a fresh game. The introductory crop override is persisted in gridPlacedMeta and stays with the crop through reload, moves and farm switches; preserve rarity and its immediate full-state saves to prevent refresh rerolls. Reuse cash-travel effects, particles, XP feedback, reduced motion and market-roll skipping. Preserve bulk-sale locking and GitHub Pages-relative asset paths. These rules were added to the end of AGENTS.md.
