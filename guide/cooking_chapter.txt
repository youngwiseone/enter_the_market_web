Chapter and controls
Mina (a nervous soup notebook keeper) visits from level 4, asking for three common carrots, then two tomatoes and an onion. After the player has reached the $25,000 cash milestone (current cash OR its existing claimed ID/flag) and at least eight unlocked/harvested produce species, her restaurant trial asks for three potatoes, three carrots and two onions. Completing the trial earns a personal gift on the next in-game day. The player explicitly receives the pot and three recipes; old saves are never auto-completed or treated as fresh.

Story requests allow 24 in-game days. Partial donations persist across reload, decline and expiry; completed stages remain earned. One accepted target at a time; fixed advertised rewards pay only on full delivery. Give rejects unrelated, immature, duplicate, stale or excess selections, including mixed selections that contain growing crops. Sell remains independent. The delivery target includes its acceptance day, so an old action cannot redirect to a later visit. A 400ms post-request guard prevents a double tap on a disappearing mobile Give control from landing on Rest.

Pot selection uses mature produce only, exact crop instance and source farm IDs. Cancel/Escape/tool/farm/day/request changes safely clear transient selection; a reload also cancels it. A wrong/repeated ingredient tap spends nothing. The final ingredient's tile receives one dish, even on a completely full grid. The combination settles synchronously before cosmetic effects. The final-tap context previews candidate meal values and carried-cost profits; eligible output tiles have exact hover quotes. No dish is an ingredient, and meals cannot be dismantled.

Balance model
- Garden Soup: carrot + potato. Tomato Stew: tomato + onion + potato. Pumpkin Mash: pumpkin + garlic + potato.
- Frozen meal value = 1.15 × SUM(input crop's canonical base price × its crop-specific rarity multiplier × its source farm sale multiplier). Market prices are excluded. All bonuses enter once; the final dish never gets another rarity/farm multiplier.
- Display quality = floor(arithmetic mean of rarity tier indexes), with common=0, uncommon=1, rare=2, mythic=3. Quality is descriptive and does not multiply value. No recooking prevents compounding.
- Costs = sum of each exact input's known seed/acquisition cost and known paid treatment cost. Unknown legacy treatment cost remains unknown. Profit previews say known-cost profit where necessary. Full numeric precision is stored; two decimals are presentation only.
- One energy per meal, contributing to cumulative dayEnergySpent even across level refills. Two cooking XP through the existing capped level system; no harvest, sale or quest-delivery XP is silently bundled into cooking.
- Bea requests soup ($8) or pumpkin mash ($40); Rowan requests tomato stew ($12). Meal visits allow 18 in-game days. Dialogue variants advance on that villager's completed meals.
- Restaurant offers require a total of 6/9/12 potatoes and tomatoes for <35/35–59/60+ unlocked tiles across both farms. Advertised payment is 125% of base produce value, frozen when accepted, without premium rarity bonuses. Twenty-four in-game days to deliver. Mina waits at least eight days after completion/decline/expiry, and the first restaurant visit waits eight days after the gift. There is no unlimited restaurant conversion loop. Premium ingredients and market peaks can beat both cooking and fixed request offers.

Persistence and integration
player.cookingChapter is additive. Existing cash, themes, crops, metadata, growth, rarities, both farms and claimed goals are retained. Stable dish IDs append to runtime items without disturbing saved/default crop ordering, and are excluded from purchasable utility rows. Existing cost and immutable crop-sale snapshots drive dish quotes. Single/bulk dish sales settle via the existing sale sequence; no crop harvest XP/count or produce market pressure.

etm.chapterSettlement.v1 journals player/farms/tools and progression-related keys as a durable recovery point before multi-key saves. Startup replays it before normal initialization. Later ordinary saves advance the checkpoint, so a meal sold after an interrupted save cannot reappear. It clears only after all included keys match. A failed journal write restores the pre-operation state and rebinds active farm arrays.
